MANUAL_COLLECTION_SPACE:
Original post:
A recently admitted Berkeley student asked for freshman dorm recommendations. They were considering Unit 1 because it is close to campus and has a social reputation, Unit 3 because it is older but has dining nearby, Clark Kerr because it is farther from campus but has nicer buildings and possibly good food, and Unit 2 because it is close to campus but they had concerns about the People’s Park area.

Selected student advice:
One student recommended applying for Blackwell anyway, saying, “You miss 100% of the shots you don’t take.” They suggested a possible ranking of Blackwell double, Unit 1 double, Unit 3 double, Clark Kerr double, and then “any building, any size” as the final choice. They also warned that the student may still receive the broad final option and should not get too fixated on a specific dorm.

Another student said that, based on their own and friends’ experiences, the ranking may not matter much because Berkeley can place students wherever housing decides.

Another student gave a similar warning, saying that none of their freshman preferences included Unit 3 or a triple, but they were still assigned a Unit 3 triple. Because of that, they chose to live off campus instead.

One commenter said People’s Park no longer exists, so Unit 2 is a better option than the original poster feared.

There was also discussion about roommates. One commenter said housing may place students and their roommate with someone random because many students have roommates. The original poster replied that they had a close friend also committing, so they hoped to avoid a fully random roommate situation. They also said they toured Unit 3 during Cal Day and felt that a triple seemed very small.
