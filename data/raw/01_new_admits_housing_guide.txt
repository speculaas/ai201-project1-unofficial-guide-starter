SUMMARY:
A student guide for new admits discussing UC Berkeley on-campus housing, the idea that freshmen use official residence halls initially, and that students often need to search for other housing after the first year. A comment adds that the housing guarantee depends on applying by the deadline and selecting broad preferences such as any building/any room configuration.

SHORT_EXCERPT_OR_NOTES:
The post says first-year students may not stay in the same dorm all four years and introduces official residence halls first. A comment notes the freshman guarantee depends on applying before the deadline and keeping the last choice broad.

MANUAL_COLLECTION_SPACE:
Selected student advice:

One commenter said the freshman housing guarantee depends on applying by the housing deadline and keeping housing preferences broad. They said students should apply before the deadline and include a broad final preference such as “any building / any room configuration.” They warned that if students do not meet those conditions, the housing guarantee may not apply.

Another commenter asked whether this means students must first submit their Statement of Intent to Register or enrollment decision and then submit housing forms to be guaranteed a spot.

A third commenter suggested an off-campus housing website for students looking in a hurry. They said the site had options one to two blocks from campus, but this should be treated as one student recommendation rather than official UC Berkeley housing guidance.

Overall, this thread is useful mainly for showing how students discuss the housing guarantee and broad preferences. For official policy details such as exact deadlines, CalNet requirements, and housing guarantee conditions, the official UC Berkeley housing application FAQ should be treated as the stronger source.
