Original post:
A prospective UC Berkeley student with priority housing from the Regents and Chancellor’s scholarship asked for advice choosing between Clark Kerr and Unit 1. The student and two friends planned to live in a triple, which ruled out Blackwell for them. They wanted a social dorm, easy access to things to do, and were interested in Greek life. They had heard mixed opinions about Clark Kerr’s distance from campus and wanted more insight.

Selected student advice:
One student said Clark Kerr and Unit 1 can both be social, but Clark Kerr has more of a “Greek life” feel because many students who join Greek life live there during freshman year. They said Unit 1 and Unit 2 can vary by floor: some floors are very social, while others are quieter.

For access to things to do, the same student said Unit 1 is much better. They said Clark Kerr can feel far away when students want to get a quick late-night bite to eat with roommates. Their summary was: choose Clark Kerr if social life and Greek life are the priority, but choose Unit 1 if location and convenience are the priority.

The same student also said Clark Kerr rooms are much nicer and bigger than rooms in the Units. Some Clark Kerr triples may have three single beds, while a Unit 1 triple can be the same size as a double room but with a bunk bed and a lofted bed.

Another student who lived in Clark Kerr said it is somewhat true that Clark Kerr is a social dorm, but many of the social elements are connected to nearby fraternities. They said this is a positive if a student wants Greek life, but a negative if they do not. Aside from fraternities and nearby fire trails, they felt there were not many fun things to do around Clark Kerr. They said the distance to campus for classes can be rough, though students get used to it. They agreed that Clark Kerr rooms are bigger, but felt the Clark Kerr dining hall was only average.

The same Clark Kerr resident said Unit 1 has more food and activities nearby. Their advice was that Unit 1 is better for most students unless the student strongly wants Greek life, strongly values room or personal space, or prefers a quieter/suburban feeling over a city-like environment.

Another student said Unit 1 is more convenient because it is closer to campus, while Clark Kerr has sports facilities. This student felt Blackwell was less social, but another student disagreed and said Blackwell can still be social depending on whether students connect with people on their floor.

A final commenter strongly preferred Clark Kerr, saying it is the best and most social dorm, especially for students interested in Greek life. They said the walk is not bad, described Clark Kerr as prettier than the Units, and said the walk can even be pleasant.

Overall, this thread shows a clear tradeoff. Clark Kerr is often described as larger, prettier, more connected to Greek life, and possibly more social for students who want that environment. Unit 1 is described as more convenient, closer to campus, closer to food, and better for students who want easy access to campus and nearby activities.
