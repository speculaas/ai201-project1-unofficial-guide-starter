Selected student advice:

One student advised that Clark Kerr is much farther from campus than the Units. They described Clark Kerr as being over six blocks away from campus, around a 20+ minute walk from the main gate. In comparison, they said Unit 1 and Unit 2 are only about a 5–10 minute walk from Sather Gate.

The student said that although Clark Kerr may have good food, in their opinion the food was not worth the longer walk. They explained that living in the Units made it easier to go back and forth between classes and the dorm during the day. They felt that if they lived in Clark Kerr, they would probably leave early in the morning and not return until late at night because of the distance.

Another student recommended ranking Blackwell first. They described Blackwell as the best dorm because it is right in front of campus, across from Cafe 3, less than five minutes from Crossroads dining hall, completely new, and has nice lounges and a gym.

Overall, this thread suggests that students comparing Unit 1, Unit 2, Clark Kerr, and Blackwell often weigh location against building quality and amenities. Unit 1 and Unit 2 are described as more convenient for walking to campus and returning between classes. Clark Kerr may have food and a different campus feel, but distance is a major downside. Blackwell is described as the strongest option if a student can get it because it is newer, close to campus, and has better amenities.
